OneSyncERP ("the app") is business software: it lets a business and its staff run accounts, sales, purchases, stock, payroll and reports. It is provided by ⟨legal company name⟩, ⟨address⟩ ("we"). Contact: ⟨privacy email address⟩.

## Who holds your data

The app does not have a cloud of its own. It connects to **your business's OneSyncERP server**, the address of which you enter when you sign in. That server is run by your business or by a hosting provider your business chose. The records you see, enter and print (customers, invoices, stock, accounts, staff details) are stored on that server and are controlled by your business, which decides who may use them, how long they are kept and where they are backed up. If you have a question about those records, ask your business's administrator.

## What the app itself keeps on your phone

* The server address and business code you entered, and your sign-in session (so you stay signed in), stored in the app's private storage.
* Preferences such as language and layout.

The app is online only and does not keep a copy of your business records on the phone. Android's own backup of the app is switched off. Signing out removes the session from the phone.

## What we collect

* **Through the app: nothing about you.** There is no advertising, no tracking, no analytics or crash-reporting service, and no account with us. We do not receive your business records.
* **Through our own services** (for example if you contact support, or if we host your business's server on its behalf): the details you give us, such as name, email address, phone number, business name and GSTIN, used to answer you, provide the service and meet legal obligations. When we host a server for a business we act on that business's instructions.

## Permissions

The app asks for network access only. It does not ask for your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or files.

## Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We share it only with service providers who help us run the service (hosting, email) under contract, or when the law requires it.

## Security

Connections between the app and the server use HTTPS. Passwords are stored only as salted hashes on the server, sign-in can require a second factor, and access is limited by the role each person is given. No system is perfectly secure; please tell us at once if you think an account has been misused.

## Keeping and deleting data

The app does not let anyone create an account for themselves: a business's administrator creates, changes and disables its users. To have your user removed, ask your administrator. To have data we hold ourselves (not on your business's server) corrected or deleted, write to ⟨privacy email⟩; we will answer within 30 days. Accounting and tax records may have to be kept for the period the law requires.

## Children

The app is for businesses and is not directed at children under 18.

## Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date above and, for a material change, tell business administrators.
