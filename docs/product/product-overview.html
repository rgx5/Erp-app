1. What OneSyncERP is

OneSyncERP runs the day-to-day of your business in one place: the billing counter, the godown, the purchase desk, the accountant's books, the GST filing and the payroll. Everything is connected, so a sale at the counter updates stock, accounts and tax automatically – no re-typing, no reconciling between separate tools.

Built for India

3. Billing counter (Point of Sale)

Designed for speed at the counter: scan, bill, print, next customer.

  • Fast item entry – scan a barcode, type a code or name; the item list is held on the counter itself so search is instant, even with thousands of items and no connection.
  • Flexible payment – cash, card, UPI and other modes, split payments across several modes, change calculation.
  • Discounts and prices – line and bill discounts, MRP-based pricing, customer-specific price lists, coupons.
  • Customer loyalty – earn and redeem points, store credit, customer look-up by phone number.
  • Hold and resume bills – park a bill while the customer fetches another item and bring it back.
  • Returns and exchanges – return against the original bill with refund or store credit.
  • Gift cards – sell a card at the counter (held as money owed to the holder, not income), print its slip, and spend it as a payment method; a balance can never be overspent.
  • Weighing scale – read the weight of loose goods straight from a serial scale.
  • Customer-facing display – a second screen that shows the customer their bill and total as it is rung up.
  • Cash drawer – open the drawer from the counter (printer-connected drawers).
  • Refunds onto gift cards – a return can be refunded as a new gift card.
  • Day-end report – bills, returns, takings by payment mode, best sellers and each shift’s cash result, printable on the till roll.
  • Receipt printers – 80 mm and 58 mm rolls.
  • Shift management – open and close shifts with opening cash, closing count and difference reporting, per-terminal.
  • Staff control – counter PINs for cashiers; manager approval needed for sensitive actions such as large discounts.
  • Receipts – silent one-click printing to a receipt printer, A4 invoices, and sharing the receipt on WhatsApp.
  • Offline selling – keep billing with no internet; sales are queued safely on the counter and uploaded automatically, with duplicate protection so nothing is billed twice.
  • Multiple counters – several terminals per location, each with its own shift.

4. Barcodes and labels

  • Scan anything – use the manufacturer's barcode, or your own; an item can carry several barcodes.
  • Generate barcodes – one click creates a valid in-store barcode (EAN-13) for an item that has none, with no risk of clashing with a manufacturer's code.
  • Bulk label printing – pick any number of items, choose how many labels of each, and print them all in one go. Missing barcodes can be created automatically at the same time.
  • Price labels – item name, MRP and a scannable barcode, in 50×25 mm and 38×25 mm roll sizes or an A4 sheet of 24.
  • Works with any scanner – USB and Bluetooth scanners that act like a keyboard need no setup.

5. Items, customers and suppliers

  • Item master – codes, names, categories, brands, units of measure, HSN/SAC, GST rate, MRP, selling and purchase price, minimum stock, active/inactive.
  • Tracking options – plain items, batch-tracked (with expiry) or serial-numbered items.
  • Item categories and brands, retail presets for common trades (grocery, apparel, pharmacy and others).
  • Item photos – one photo per item.
  • Variants – create every size and colour of a style in one step, each with its own stock, price and barcode.
  • Price lists – retail, wholesale, dealer or any custom list, with effective dates.
  • Customers and suppliers – contact details, GSTIN, GST treatment, credit terms, addresses, one record for a party that is both buyer and seller.
  • TDS on supplier payments – tick "Deduct TDS" when paying a supplier: the section's rate is worked out (below the limit nothing, without a PAN at least 20%), the bank pays the rest, the tax is held in TDS payable, and a register shows what is due to be deposited by the 7th of the next month. Record the challan to post the deposit, and download the register for the quarterly TDS return. Sections, rates and limits are editable.
  • GSTR-2B check and annual working – choose the GSTR-2B file downloaded from the GST portal and every supplier invoice is checked against your purchase bills (matched, figures differ, possible match, in 2B only, in books only), with CSV. An annual (GSTR-9) working adds the twelve monthly GSTR-3B figures for the year.
  • Bank statements from any file – CSV, Excel, OFX/QFX, QIF or MT940; the bank's heading and summary rows are skipped and opening and closing balances are read where the file has them.
  • Group statements – with more than one company, a group trial balance puts the companies side by side; tick inter-company accounts to take them out of the group totals.
  • Lead management – enquiries from Facebook and Instagram lead ads, Google Ads lead forms, your website and any other system arrive as leads within seconds, each with its campaign, ad and click details. People can also add or import leads by hand. Leads are shared out to the team by turns or by rules (city, PIN code, source, campaign), with a response-time clock that flags a lead nobody has called. A board and a list follow each lead through your own stages with calls, notes and follow-up tasks; a repeat enquirer is kept as one lead; one click makes the lead a customer and starts a quotation. Reports show leads, wins, sales and cost per lead and per sale by source and campaign, from advert spend you enter. Solar fields (monthly bill, roof, system size, DISCOM) are built in. Needs the server to be reachable from the internet; Meta live ads need Meta's own approval for lead retrieval.
  • Customer and supplier screens – click any customer or supplier for their own page: what they owe you (or you owe them), overdue amount, credit limit, and tabs with the full ledger and every document with them: invoices and notes, receipts, quotations, sales orders and challans; bills, payments, purchase orders and goods received. Rows open the document; New invoice and New bill start with that party chosen.
  • Bulk import – bring items and parties from CSV, Excel (.xlsx), JSON, XML, database files such as a Vyapar backup (.vyb), or a zip of these. Columns are matched for you and can be changed, units, tax rates and party types from the other software are translated, and every row is checked with an error report before anything is saved. Transactions, balances and opening stock are not imported.
  • Quick find – a command palette (Ctrl+K) to jump to any screen, item or party.

6. Sales

  • Tax invoices and bills of supply – correct document for regular, composition and exempt supplies.
  • Credit and debit notes – for returns, price corrections and adjustments, linked to the original invoice.
  • Quotations and sales orders – printable, priced with the same GST as the final invoice; convert a quote to an order and an order to an invoice without re-entry.
  • Proforma invoices – a clearly marked “not a tax invoice” document made from any quote or order, with an optional UPI QR so the customer can pay in advance. Nothing is posted to the books.
  • Delivery challans – record goods sent out before billing, with vehicle number and transporter; print the challan, then invoice it in full or in parts. Stock leaves and GST is charged when you invoice.
  • Recurring invoices – rent, subscriptions and maintenance contracts raised automatically on their due dates (weekly to yearly), with a history and a clear message if one cannot be raised.
  • Payment reminders – a list of overdue customers with ready-written WhatsApp messages (opened from your own WhatsApp), editable wording, and a record of who was chased and when.
  • Attachments – attach scanned bills, signed delivery copies or purchase order copies to invoices, orders, quotes, bills and purchase orders.
  • Pay by scanning – invoices can carry a UPI QR code for the exact amount.
  • Sales analysis – sales and profit by item, category, brand, day, cashier or customer, with returns netted off.
  • Customer receipts – record payments, allocate across invoices, part payments and advances.
  • Receivables and ageing – who owes what and for how long (current, 30, 60, 90+ days).
  • Tax handled automatically – CGST/SGST or IGST by place of supply, tax-inclusive or tax-exclusive pricing, SEZ and exports treatment.
  • E-invoice (IRN) and e-way bill, file based – the software checks each invoice and prepares the government's own upload file (e-invoice and e-way bill bulk JSON). You upload it on the portal, then load the portal's reply back: the IRN, acknowledgement and signed QR code are stored and print on the invoice, and the e-way bill number is recorded. An invoice with a live IRN cannot be cancelled until the IRN is cancelled on the portal and recorded here. No portal login or provider account is used, and nothing is sent to the government from the software.
  • Print preview and direct printing – labels are previewed before printing and can go straight to a chosen printer; PDFs open at once in the computer's own PDF viewer and are kept in Documents.
  • Printable, branded documents – invoice layout with your name, GSTIN, bank details and terms.

7. Purchasing

  • Purchase orders – raise, approve and track against supplier.
  • Goods receipts – receive full or partial deliveries into a warehouse.
  • Supplier bills – enter bills with GST, and a three-way match against order and receipt to catch quantity or price mismatches before payment.
  • Payments to suppliers – pay one or many bills, part payments, advances.
  • Payables and ageing – what you owe and when it is due.
  • Debit notes – return goods or claim price corrections.
  • Landed cost – add freight, duty and handling to the true cost of goods so margins are real.
  • Input tax credit – eligible and blocked credit tracked per bill and carried to GST returns.

8. Inventory

  • Stock on hand – by item and warehouse, with reserved and available quantity.
  • Stock movements – a complete trail of every receipt, sale, transfer and adjustment.
  • Multiple warehouses and locations – and transfers between them with in-transit tracking.
  • Stock counts – physical verification with variance review and posting of adjustments.
  • Batch and serial tracking – batch numbers with expiry, serial numbers, quality holds.
  • Valuation – stock value reports using standard costing methods, tied to the accounts.
  • Reservations – stock set aside for confirmed sales orders.
  • Reorder levels – set a level and quantity per item; a reorder list shows what is low (less what is already on order) and turns it into a purchase order in one click.

9. Accounting

Every sale, purchase, stock movement and payroll run posts to the books automatically. Your accountant sees clean, balanced entries.

  • Chart of accounts – ready-made Indian structure, add your own accounts.
  • Journal entries – manual entries with balance checks and audit trail.
  • Financial statements – Trial Balance, Profit & Loss, Balance Sheet in Schedule III format, ledgers and day book; export to Excel/CSV.
  • Periods and year-end – open and close months, lock closed periods.
  • Bank reconciliation – match bank statement lines to book entries.
  • Expenses – record and categorise day-to-day expenses with GST credit where eligible.
  • Multiple companies – separate books per legal entity (Enterprise and above).

10. GST and statutory compliance

  • GST registration types – regular, composition, SEZ and others, with multiple registrations (states) per company.
  • GSTR-1 – outward supplies prepared and exported as portal-ready JSON.
  • GSTR-3B – summary return with tax payable and input credit.
  • ITC register – input tax credit eligibility and reversals.
  • CMP-08 – quarterly statement for composition taxpayers.
  • Report centre – all statutory and management reports in one place, filterable and exportable.

You upload the prepared files to the GST portal yourself; OneSyncERP produces accurate, ready-to-upload data.

11. Payroll

  • Employee master – salary structure, bank details, statutory numbers.
  • Monthly payroll runs – earnings, deductions, attendance-based pay, review before posting.
  • PF, ESI and Professional Tax – calculated per statutory rules and slabs.
  • Payslips – printable per employee.
  • Statutory dues – what is payable to each authority, and remittance tracking.
  • Accounting posting – salaries and dues flow to the books.

12. Manufacturing

  • Bills of material – components and quantities per finished product, with scrap allowance.
  • Work centres – machines or stages with their cost rates.
  • Work orders – plan, issue materials, record output, close.
  • Production costing – material, labour and overhead cost per batch; variance against standard.
  • Job work / subcontracting – send material to a job worker, receive finished goods, track pending challans and charges.

13. Control, security and administration

  • Users and roles – define who can see and do what, down to individual actions; ready-made roles for owner, accountant, cashier, store keeper.
  • Approvals – high-value or sensitive documents wait for a manager's OK (Enterprise and above).
  • Your data is yours – download a complete copy of all your records in one file at any time (owner-level, audited), check later that a saved file is whole and unchanged, and have it restored into a new business by your administrator.
  • Notifications – a bell shows approvals waiting, low stock, overdue customers, shifts left open, recurring invoices and an expiring licence.
  • Languages – menus, the counter and the headings on invoices and receipts in Hindi, Marathi or Gujarati (printed documents show both languages).
  • Audit log – who did what and when, for every change; cannot be edited.
  • Two-step sign-in (MFA) and automatic lock-out after repeated wrong passwords.
  • Tenant isolation – each client's data is separated at the database level; one business can never see another's.
  • Guided setup – a setup wizard for company, GST, locations and opening data, with industry templates to start fast.
  • Integration keys – secure API keys for connecting other systems.
  • Easy, modern interface – clean screens, keyboard shortcuts, accessible colours and contrast.

16. What is not included today

To set honest expectations, the following are not in the current version:

  • Sending sales back to Meta and Google as conversions, and sending WhatsApp messages to leads automatically (WhatsApp opens from your own number)
  • Sending e-invoices and e-way bills to the government portals directly from the software (today you upload the prepared file yourself; e-way bills for delivery challans are not prepared)
  • Direct filing to the GST portal (GSTR-2B and annual-return working are provided as checks)
  • Automatic bank feeds from the bank (statements are imported from files)
  • Multiple currencies
  • Consolidated profit and loss and balance sheet across companies (a group trial balance with eliminations is provided)
  • TCS handling on sales, TDS on bills and income (TDS is deducted on supplier payments), and generating the TDS return file
  • Batch/serial-tracked items can only be sold while online at the counter
  • Offline returns at the counter
  • Sending reminders by SMS or email from inside the software (WhatsApp opens from your own phone number)
  • Translated screens beyond the menus, the counter and printed headings; amounts in words stay in English
  • Two-way sync between several offline sites (planned)
  • Mobile app for staff (the owner dashboard works in a phone browser)
