How we understand your business

This is what we heard from you. If we have got any of it wrong, please mark it up and send it back.

1

An enquiry comes in

From an advertisement, a reference or a channel partner. Today it sits in a phone, a notebook or a sheet, and follow-ups depend on someone remembering.

2

Someone visits the site

The roof, the shade, the electricity bill and the sanctioned load are noted down, usually on paper or in photos scattered across phones.

3

A proposal is prepared

A long, branded document that takes real time to put together, in a separate tool, with prices kept somewhere else.

4

The customer says yes

An advance is taken, and the paperwork starts: electricity company application, net meter, and the subsidy.

5

Material is bought and the crew goes out

Panels, inverters, structures and cables are ordered. Everything has to reach the right site, and the serial numbers have to be recorded for the warranty.

6

The system is switched on, and billed

Payment comes in stages. The subsidy arrives weeks later, to the customer, and you need to know when.

7

Then the customer needs you again

Cleaning, a fault, a warranty claim, a yearly maintenance contract. That is where a good name is built, and it is easy to lose track of.

The common thread: each step is handled well by someone, but the information doesn't travel with the customer. We want a customer's whole story, from first call to last visit, to be one click away.

What we will build for you

Everything below is made around the way a solar company works. The same people, the same customers and the same prices show up wherever you look.

1

Enquiries and site visits

Every enquiry goes in one list with its source, who is handling it, and when to call next. A site visit form on the phone captures the roof, shade, electricity bill and photos in one go, so nothing is retyped later.

2

Proposals that look the part

Choose the system size, and the proposal is built for you: what is included, the expected savings and payback, the subsidy, the finance option, and your branding. Several versions of the same proposal can sit side by side, and it can go to the customer as a PDF or on WhatsApp.

3

Quotations from ready-made kits

Set up your 3 kW, 5 kW or 10 kW packages once, with the panels, inverter, structure and cables you usually use. A quotation then takes a minute, and your margin on each one is visible to you.

4

Projects, step by step

Once an order is confirmed it becomes a project with its own checklist: survey, design, electricity company application, material, installation, commissioning, net meter, handover. You see at a glance what is stuck, and where.

5

Subsidy tracking

For every customer: which scheme, the application number, the current status, the amount expected and the date it arrived. No more asking around about who is still waiting.

6

Documents and photos in one place

Application forms, certificates, test reports, and the installation photos the subsidy process asks for, all filed against the customer's project, ready to download when needed.

7

Stock and purchases

See what is in your godown and what is set aside for which customer. Panels and inverters are recorded by serial number, so a warranty question is answered in seconds. Orders to your suppliers, and what you owe them, are tracked as well.

8

Billing in stages

Advance, material delivery, commissioning: each invoice is raised at the right moment, with GST handled the way your accountant needs it. You always know who has paid, who hasn't, and how long it has been.

9

Cheques, bank loans and payments

Customer cheques are followed until they clear. For customers on a bank loan, you see what is approved, released and pending.

10

Accounts and GST

Sales, purchases and expenses flow into your books on their own. Your accountant gets clean records and the working for your GST returns, without retyping.

11

After-sales and yearly service

Yearly maintenance contracts, cleaning visits and complaints are scheduled and tracked. Warranty claims on the manufacturer are logged with the serial numbers ready.

12

Money made, project by project

What each project earned after material, labour and commission. Plus the numbers you check every week: enquiries and conversions, kilowatts sold and installed, subsidies pending, projects waiting for a net meter, and payments due.

For your site team: a simple phone app for the people on the roof. They fill the site visit, take photos, tick the installation checklist and get the customer's signature on handover. It also works when the signal is poor, and sends everything across later.

What you won't see: factory production, counter sales and other things that belong to other kinds of businesses. We don't switch them on. What you will see on day one: your own customers, items and open balances, brought across from your present billing program or sheets.
